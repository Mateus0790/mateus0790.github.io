Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This personal tool is operated by Mateus (GitHub: Mateus0790) and is used only with his own TikTok account.

Data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the tool accesses basic TikTok profile information, account statistics, the list of published videos, and permission to upload and publish videos.

How data is used

The data is used only to publish videos and view statistics for the owner's own account. It is not sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored by the integration service used to connect to TikTok (Composio) and can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings under "Manage app permissions".

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Mateus0790/mateus0790.github.io.
